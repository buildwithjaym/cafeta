## What is CAFÉTA?

**CAFÉTA is a local café and milk-tea discovery platform.**

In simple words:

> **CAFÉTA helps people find where to get coffee or milk tea, see what other people experienced there, and discover new places around them.**

The name comes from:

**CAFÉ + TA = CAFÉTA**
Think: **“Kape tayo.” / “Café tayo.”**

The main idea is not just **“find the nearest café.”**

It is:

> **“Where should we go for coffee?” → Check CAFÉTA.**

CAFÉTA starts in **Basilan**, with the possibility of expanding to other places later.

---

# Main Features of CAFÉTA

For the first version, I would keep it to these **7 core features**.

### 1. Explore Map

The heart of CAFÉTA.

Users open a map and see nearby:

* Coffee shops
* Milk-tea shops
* New places
* Popular places

Tap a marker → see a small preview → open the café.

### 2. Search & Filters

Search for:

> “coffee near me”
> “milk tea”
> “Café ABC”

Keep filters simple:

**Coffee | Milk Tea | Open Now | Nearby**

Don't add 15+ filters yet.

### 3. Café Profiles

Every place gets its own page containing:

* Cover photo
* Business name
* Coffee/Milk Tea category
* Location
* Distance
* Opening hours
* Price range
* Short description
* Menu
* Community Moments
* Save button
* Directions

### 4. CAFÉTA Moments

This should be your special feature.

Instead of traditional long reviews, users share a **Moment**.

For example:

> 📷 *photo of iced coffee*
>
> **“Found this after class. Their Spanish latte was worth the walk.”**
>
> @jayms · Café ABC

Each Moment contains:

**1 photo + short caption + café/location**

This helps someone understand **what the experience at the café is actually like**.

### 5. Discover

A simple discovery feed with sections such as:

**Near You**
**Trending Now**
**New Places**
**Hidden Gems**
**Recent Moments**

This gives people a reason to browse even when they don't know what café they want.

### 6. Saved Places

Users tap ♥ to save places.

Call the page something simple like:

### **Saved**

Inside:

**Want to Visit**
**Favorites**

You could introduce custom collections later.

### 7. User Profile

A simple profile:

**@username**

**12** Places Discovered
**6** Moments

Then show:

**Moments | Saved**

Don't build followers, messaging, complicated XP systems, etc. yet.

Think of them as two different pieces:

**OpenStreetMap** provides the geographic/map data.

**MapLibre** is the library you use to display and interact with the map in your Next.js application.

So your architecture becomes:

**CAFÉTA**
↓
**MapLibre**
↓
**Map tiles/provider based on OpenStreetMap data**

Why MapLibre? It's open-source, customizable, works well for a custom map experience, and avoids locking the core UI into a proprietary map SDK.

One important technical note: don't treat the public OpenStreetMap tile servers as free production hosting for unlimited traffic. For an MVP you can start carefully, but choose a proper OSM-compatible tile provider as usage grows.

# CAFÉTA Navbar

Don't overload the navigation.

For **desktop**, I'd use:

**CAFÉTA Logo | Explore | Discover | Saved | + Share a Moment | Profile**

For **mobile**, I would make it even simpler with a bottom navigation:

**Explore | Discover | + | Saved | Profile**

The center **+** is for creating a Moment.

---

# 1. EXPLORE

This should be the **default/main page**.

### Navbar

**Explore**

### Page headline

> **Where to, today?**

Supporting text:

> Discover coffee and milk tea around you.

Then:

**[ Search cafés, milk tea, or places... ]**

Quick filters:

`Coffee` `Milk Tea` `Open Now` `Nearby`

And then the large interactive map.

### Map markers

☕ = Coffee
🧋 = Milk Tea

You don't necessarily need literal emoji markers in the final UI; custom CAFÉTA marker icons would look more premium.

> Open until 9 PM
>
> 8 recent Moments
>

That's enough.

# What about business owners?

Don't put **Business Dashboard** in the normal user navbar.

At the bottom of a business profile:

> **Own this business?**
>
> Claim this place to manage information, photos, menu and opening hours.
>
> **Claim Business →**

Once approved, the owner gets a separate simple dashboard:

**Overview | Business Info | Menu | Photos | Community**

No complicated analytics system initially.

↓

**Explore Map**

↓

**Discover a Café**

↓

**Visit**
